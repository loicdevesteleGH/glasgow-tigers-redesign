Since 1946

A home at Ashfield

Speedway has raced in Glasgow since 1946, at Hampden, White City and Shawfield before settling at Ashfield.

At the end of 2014 the club risked closing. Local businessman Gerry Facenna, whose company Allied Vehicles is based nearby, bought the stadium, cleared the club's debts and invested £2 million to turn a run-down venue into the Allied Ashfield Stadium.

"We aren't in this to make money from it. Any profits we make will go back into the club and the community."
Gerry Facenna, owner
