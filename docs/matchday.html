Getting here

Allied Ashfield Stadium

404 Hawthorn Street, Springburn
Glasgow G22 6RU

About 10 minutes from the city centre

Take the Glasgow Queen Street to Anniesland service and get off at Ashfield. The stadium is a short walk from the station.

Two direct routes

  • 75
    Castlemilk / Milton

    Operated by First Bus

  • 90
    Partick / Braehead

    Operated by First Bus

Plan with First Bus

Sat nav: G22 6RU

From the M8 (south):

  1. Exit just past Junction 15 (A803, Kirkintilloch), keeping right on the slip road.
  2. Turn right at the lights, under the M8 flyover, and follow signs for Springburn and Port Dundas.
  3. Join Springburn Road and go straight through three sets of lights.
  4. Keep left up the hill and take the first slip road at the top.
Full directions from every route
Parking

Where to park

£5

Premium parking

At the stadium, closest to the gates. First come, first served.

£3

Hawthorn Street

Just down the road, opposite Mobility Solutions. The budget-friendly option.

Blue badge

Accessible bays

Dedicated bays at the stadium, with an access ramp into the ground.

Please park considerately. Streets around the stadium are residential. Don't block driveways, and leave room for residents to come and go.

Accessibility

Everyone's welcome

  • Blue badge parking at the stadium
  • Ramp access from the car park into the ground

Need something specific? Call us before race night and we'll help plan your visit.
